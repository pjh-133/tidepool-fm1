Tidepool FM-1 emulator

The Shallows bank running on the C port, on a to-scale FM-1 front panel. Drag a knob up or down (or scroll over it), and click the keys and buttons. The sound is the port's own; the CPU figure is an estimate from the Mac benchmark.

Or press any key on the panel.

What the controls do

White keysThe 16 loops, left to right in rows of four: Drones, Textures, Melodies, Beats. The key light is on while a loop plays and blinks while it waits for the next beat.
KNOB1–4The selected setting for the four loops in the selected row. The row's bars on the screen show the values.
OCT− / OCT+Which setting the knobs change: Volume, Attack, Decay, Reverb, Echo, Pan (shown at the bottom left of the screen).
ALGORITHMWhich row the knobs control. Pressing a key also selects its row.
SELECTTempo, 56–140 bpm.
MASTERMain volume.
PLAY/STOPStops everything. Press again to bring back the same mix.
HOME (hold 3 s)Surprise me: a random mix, as in the web app. A countdown shows while you hold it; let go early and nothing happens.
PRESETSBank. Only Shallows is ported.
Black keys, other buttonsNot used in this sketch: the screen says so.

Computer keys: 1–4 Q–R A–F Z–V loops, Space PLAY/STOP, [ ] row, , . setting, - = tempo. A focused knob turns with the arrow keys.
